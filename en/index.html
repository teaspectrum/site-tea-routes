The Ancient Tea Horse RoadA network of trade routes linking southwest China's tea regions with the Tibetan plateau and South Asia, shaped by horse caravans, Pu-erh tea and multi-ethnic exchange from the Tang to the Qing.The Ancient Tea Road of Southwest ChinaA network of pack-horse and porter routes linking the tea districts of Sichuan, Guizhou and Yunnan, from Mengding Mountain through the karst plateau to Kunming and the Pu'er tea country.The Arabian Tea RouteA maritime and overland trade network linking Indian Ocean tea producers with Arabian Peninsula, Red Sea and Levantine markets. Indian black tea and Chinese green tea moved through Mumbai, Aden, Mocha, Mecca, Damascus, Cairo, Muscat and Dubai.The Brazil Paraná Yerba Mate RoadA thematic cultural corridor through Paraná's yerba mate districts, Curitiba's trading houses, the port of Paranaguá and the tri-border area at Iguaçu. Yerba mate is Ilex paraguariensis, not Camellia sinensis; the page treats the "tea road" as a reading frame rather than a single ancient caravan track.The Darjeeling Himalayan RailwayA narrow-gauge mountain railway built to carry tea and people from the Darjeeling gardens to the plains, and a UNESCO World Heritage landscape.The Dutch VOC Tea RouteThe Dutch East India Company’s maritime tea route linking Amsterdam, the Cape, Batavia, Chinese ports and Nagasaki’s Dejima.

The Ancient Tea Horse Road

A network of trade routes linking southwest China's tea regions with the Tibetan plateau and South Asia, shaped by horse caravans, Pu-erh tea and multi-ethnic exchange from the Tang to the Qing.

Intercontinental

The Great Tea Road

The overland tea trade from Fujian and the Chinese interior across Mongolia to Siberia and European Russia, centred on the Kyakhta market and managed largely by Shanxi merchants from the late seventeenth to the early twentieth century.

Intercontinental

The Maritime Tea Route

The sea route that carried Chinese tea from Fujian and Guangdong to Southeast Asia, Europe and the Americas, and later linked the tea gardens of India, Ceylon and Kenya to global markets.
